About LLMsControlDock

{{/* One line that says what it is. */}}

A local monitor and control page for Ollama.

{{/* What it does, in plain words. */}}

It shows which models are installed and which are loaded in memory, lets you load, unload, tune and delete models, tells you how to download a deleted model again, and compares how fast tested models answer.
