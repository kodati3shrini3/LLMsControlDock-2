Settings

{{/* What the page does. */}}

Check the values, change what you need and press Save settings. A value left empty (or equal to the default) uses the default. Values marked restart are used the next time the monitor starts; the others apply at once. The look of the page (light or dark, text size) is changed with the buttons at the top of the monitor page.
